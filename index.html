Who we are

About 3Point Web Solutions

We build websites that bring you customers.

3Point Web Solutions is a Nairobi-based web design and development company serving small and medium-sized businesses in Nairobi and across Kenya. We build business websites, e-commerce stores, portals and custom software that help organizations establish a professional online presence, connect with customers and grow.

We help businesses address common digital challenges, from an outdated or slow website to a poor mobile experience or the need to sell online. Our team combines website design, web development and practical digital solutions, guided by Design, Development and Results, to deliver value for each business.
